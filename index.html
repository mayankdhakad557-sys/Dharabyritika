Some Memories Deserve to Last Forever.

Wedding flowers hold the quiet essence of your most cherished celebrations. They carry the laughter, prayers, rituals, and promises made on a day that marks a new beginning. Yet, blossoms naturally fade over time.

At Dhara by Ritika, we believe those emotions shouldn't wither away. Through painstaking handcraftsmanship and crystal-clear resin, we encapsulate your original varmalas and celebratory flora—preserving their delicate beauty, texture, and sentiment so you can hold onto your favorite moments for a lifetime.

Our Story

Made With Memories in Mind.

Dhara by Ritika creates handcrafted resin pieces designed to preserve the beauty and memories behind your most meaningful moments, as well as offering a curated boutique collection.

We specialize in transforming original wedding varmalas, bridal bouquet flora, and special milestone flowers into permanent clear resin sculptures and functional art. Each piece is meticulously dried, prepared, and individually poured to preserve natural color structures and emotional worth.
